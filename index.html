Who We Are

PBAT–KOH Tertiary Education Mobilisation Alliance

The PBAT–KOH Tertiary Education Mobilisation Alliance (PBAT–KOH TEMA) is a powerful alliance dedicated to campaign mobilisation, unity, commitment, and collective political action among tertiary students and academic/non-academic staff.

PBAT–KOH TEMA seeks to foster an environment where students and staff can collaborate meaningfully, build leadership capacity, advance political participation, and champion the Renewed Hope agenda for continued progress across tertiary campuses.

Student and staff political mobilization
Leadership development & civic training
Campus captains and staff captain networks
Renewed Hope advocacy & community outreach

Our Vision

To mobilize, unite, and empower tertiary education communities with the leadership, dedication, and collaborative strength required for Renewed Hope and long-term national progress.


Our Approach

PBAT–KOH TEMA emphasizes unity, grassroots campus mobilization, active coordinator and captain leadership, structured volunteer networks, and civic engagement across all institutions.

Our Focus

What PBAT–KOH TEMA Stands For

Building an organized, connected, and empowered tertiary community through practical political and civic engagement.

Student & Staff Development

Supporting students and staff with opportunities for civic education, leadership development, networking, and mobilization experience.

Community & Campuses

Creating strong connections between students, staff, captains, institutions, and communities.

Renewed Hope Progress

Championing progress, policy awareness, and youth-centered development across all tertiary campuses.

Mobilisation & Service

Encouraging active volunteerism, campus captaincy, and responsible participation in national progress.
