Who We Are

BKSA: Students & Youth Alliance for BAT-KOH

The BKSA Students Alliance is a grassroots movement of Nigerian students, youth corps members, and young professionals dedicated to supporting the re-election of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu (BAT) and the election of KOH as Governor of Lagos State.

We believe in the power of youth votes and student mobilization to shape Nigeria's political future. Our network spans campuses across all 36 states.
